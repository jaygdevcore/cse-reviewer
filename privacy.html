Privacy Policy

CSE Reviewer PH by JayG Devcore · Last updated: October 4, 2026

CSE Reviewer PH ("the app") is a practice reviewer for the Philippine Civil Service Examination, developed by JayG Devcore ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: your study data stays on your phone, and we do not collect personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device: the first name you type in (optional), your chosen exam level and exam date, your answers, scores, mistakes, bookmarks, streaks, XP and badges, and your app settings. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Backups. If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may save this app data to your own Google account, so it can come back when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. That backup is made and kept by Google under your account, and we cannot see it; you can turn it off in your phone's settings. The app also has a Back up button (More › Settings) that creates a backup text for you to keep. It is shared only where you choose to send it, such as your own email or notes.

2. Subscriptions and payments

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card, e-wallet or bank details. The app only receives from Google Play whether your subscription is active, so it can unlock Premium features. Google's handling of your payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can cancel anytime in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

3. Internet use

The app connects to the internet only to download new practice questions from our website (hosted on GitHub Pages) and to check your subscription with Google Play. If you enter an access code, the app checks it against that same downloaded file, on your phone; the code itself is never sent anywhere. Downloading questions does not send any personal information. Like any website, the host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security; we do not use them to identify you. GitHub's handling of these logs is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, or device identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party analytics or tracking.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

5. Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for the Civil Service Examination and is not directed to children under 13.

6. Not affiliated with the government

CSE Reviewer PH is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Civil Service Commission or any government agency. Practice questions are original and modeled on the published exam coverage; actual exam questions are confidential.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
